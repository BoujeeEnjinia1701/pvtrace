# BOM notes

- Line numbers match the callouts in `media/exploded.png` and Table 1 of `docs/02-concept.md`.
- Costs are indicative single-unit prices in USD from typical online and distributor listings in September 2026. They are estimates, not quotes, and will be checked when suppliers are selected at TRL 3.
- Total: about $149 against the $150 budget in `project.yaml`, a margin of about $1.
- Proposed options that would change the total (awaiting Amish): a third load capacitor (about +$6), galvanic isolation between the measurement board and the controller (about +$8), a sun-readable transflective display (about +$10).
- Brand-matched MC4 connectors are specified on purpose. Cheaper mixed-brand connectors can overheat when mated with a module's own connectors.
- The PV module, stand and module leads shown in grey in the media are not part of the kit.
