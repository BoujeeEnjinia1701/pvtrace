# BOM notes

- Line numbers match the callouts in `media/exploded.png` and Table 1 of `docs/02-concept.md`. Line 13 (hardware and consumables) is not modeled.
- Costs are indicative single-unit prices in USD from typical online and distributor listings in September 2026. They are estimates, not quotes. Supplier types are given for every line; named distributors are examples, not selections.
- Total: USD 192.00 (checked by `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py`). Value-engineering target: USD 165 (`budget_usd` in `project.yaml`, a hypothetical control target, not a limit; Amish, 2026-10-01). Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 192, USD 27 over the target. Savings worth trying are in `docs/06-design-decisions.md`.
- Changes at TRL 3 (PVT-DDR-001): a third load capacitor on line 5 (+$6), the isolation barrier as new line 14 (+$8), a light grey case on line 1 and a charger with a temperature cut-off on line 10 at no added cost.
- Options that would change the total: a transflective display for R13 (about +$10). The fourth load capacitor for R3 is not used: Amish decided on 2026-10-02 (PVT-DEC-001, item 4) to measure the three capacitors at build and select a set of at least 6.4 mF, so line 5 now buys four capacitors and fits the best three (one spare, USD 6; the spare is not modelled). At least 6.4 mF was decided; the calculation uses 6.45 mF because 6.4 mF gives 19.9 ms, 0.1 ms short of the 20 ms bound (proposed, awaiting Amish).
- Decided by Amish on 2026-10-02 (PVT-DEC-001, item 6): a keypad and status LEDs are kept from the appearance model. Line 18 (USD 8) covers a blank 3-key membrane switch (about USD 3.50), a printed overlay label (USD 1), two LED panel clips with LEDs (about USD 2) and a header and 6-way lead (USD 1.50); the lid slot and holes are in making sketch PVT-DWG-104. Prices are indicative distributor and online retailer listings, not quotes.
- Change from PVT-DDR-002: printed PETG display sun hood as new line 15 (+$1, material only), callout 15 in the exploded view.
- Brand-matched MC4 connectors are specified on purpose. Cheaper mixed-brand connectors can overheat when mated with a module's own connectors.
- The PV module, stand and module leads shown in grey in the media are not part of the kit.
- Changes from PVT-DDR-003 (design for construction, 2026-10-01): new line 16, the polycarbonate chassis plate (+$3); new line 17, the USB-C charging socket (+$5); line 3 adds a 16-bit ADC module for the reference cell (+$3); line 12 adds the pod clip's thumb screw and an M12 gland (+$1); line 13 adds standoffs, screws, ties and gasket tape (+$2); lines 1, 2, 4 and 15 respecified at no change in price. Lines 16 and 17 are callouts 16 and 17 in the exploded view.
